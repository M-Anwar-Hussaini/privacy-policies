BitLore privacy policy

Last updated 6 November 2026

BitLore is developed by Anwar Hussaini. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information we collect

BitLore does not collect, upload or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

Information stored on your device

Your reading progress, bookmarks, quiz results, recent searches and settings are saved only on your device, so the app can remember them. They never leave your device. You can erase them at any time in Settings › Your data, or by uninstalling the app.

Read (text-to-speech)

The Read feature uses your device's own text-to-speech service. BitLore does not record audio or use the microphone.

Copying and sharing

When you copy or share a passage, its text or picture goes only to your clipboard or to the app you choose in Android's share menu. When you save a passage as a picture, it is stored in your phone's gallery, in the BitLore album. BitLore sends nothing by itself. On Android 10 and older, saving a picture asks for permission to write to your storage; it is used only for that.

Links

The developer profile contains links to email, LinkedIn, GitHub and X. Opening one leaves the app, and that service's own privacy policy applies.

Children

BitLore is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear in the app and at the same web address, with a new date.
