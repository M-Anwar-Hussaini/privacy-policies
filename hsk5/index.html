HSK 5 privacy policy

App: HSK 5 (com.anwar.hsk5) for Android · Last updated 8 November 2026

HSK 5 is developed by Anwar Hussaini. It teaches the HSK Standard Course 5 in Persian (Dari) and English. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, upload or sell any personal information. The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics of its own. All lessons are built into the app.

Information stored on your device

Your settings (language, theme, text size, voice) and your review progress for each word are saved only on your device, so the app can remember them. They never leave your device. You can erase them at any time by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Handwriting recognition

Writing practice uses Google ML Kit Digital Ink Recognition. The first time you open it, the app downloads the Chinese handwriting model from Google (this needs the internet). After that, your handwriting is read on your device and is never sent anywhere.

Like other Google ML Kit features, it may send Google basic technical information, such as the device model, Android version, app version and performance and error data, so Google can keep the feature working. It does not include your handwriting or anything you type. See Google's ML Kit terms and privacy policy.

Read aloud

Read-aloud uses your device's own text-to-speech service. HSK 5 does not record audio or use the microphone.

Notifications

When a text is playing, the app shows a media notification with play and pause buttons. On Android 13 and later it asks for permission to show it. The app sends no other notifications.

Links

The About page links to email, WhatsApp, LinkedIn and GitHub. Opening one leaves the app, and that service's own privacy policy applies.

Children

HSK 5 is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear at the same web address, with a new date.
