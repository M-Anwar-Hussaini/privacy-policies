Wisdom privacy policy

App: Wisdom (com.anwar.wisdom) for Android · Last updated: 4 November 2026

In short: Wisdom does not collect, send or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not even have permission to use the internet. Everything you do in it stays on your phone.

Who we are

Wisdom is a quote of the day app made by Anwar Hussaini, an independent developer (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Information we collect

None. Wisdom does not collect personal information, device identifiers, location, contacts, usage statistics or crash reports, and it does not share any data with third parties. All quotes are built into the app, so it never needs to download anything.

Information stored on your phone

To work, the app saves a few things in its private storage on your device. We cannot see any of it:

  • Quotes you mark as favorites
  • Quotes you write yourself
  • Which quotes you have already seen, and your daily streak
  • Your settings, such as theme, font and daily reminder time

You can delete this information at any time by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this app data in your device backup so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by us, and we have no access to it.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show your daily quote reminder, if you turn it on. You can turn it off in the app or in Android settings.
  • Run at startup: so the daily reminder and the home screen widget keep working after your phone restarts.

Some technical permissions (such as keeping the device awake briefly to deliver a scheduled notification) are added by the libraries the app uses. None of them give the app access to your personal data or the internet.

Features that use other apps

  • Sharing: when you tap Share, Android’s share menu opens and you choose where the quote or quote image goes. Only what you choose to share leaves the app, and the app you share to handles it under its own privacy policy.
  • Read aloud: quotes are spoken by the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone. The engine is provided by your phone, not by Wisdom.
  • Home screen widget: shows the quote of the day on your home screen. It uses the same on-device data as the app.

Children

Wisdom is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above before that version is released.
